# Editing your website

You can change your own details and prices — no code, no fuss. Here's how.

## Logging in

1. On your phone or computer, go to **your website address followed by `/admin`**
   (for example `https://sturrystorage.co.uk/admin`).
2. Tap **Log in** and enter your email. You'll get a code or a login link —
   follow it. That's it, you're in.

You'll see a list on the left with two things you can edit: **Business details**
and **Prices**.

## Change a price

1. Tap **Prices**.
2. Tap the one you want — 20ft container, 40ft container, or Open yard space.
3. Change **Price from (£)** — just type the number, like `40`.
4. Tap **Save** (top right).

- To hide a row from the table (say you stop offering 40ft), turn off
  **Show this row**. It stays saved, just hidden.
- If there's no set price, leave **Price from** blank and it shows your
  **"text to show when there's no price"** instead (like "Call for availability").

## Change your phone number, address or hours

1. Tap **Business details**.
2. Change what you need:
   - **Phone number (as shown)** is what people see.
   - **Phone number (for the Call button)** is what the button dials — keep the
     `+44` format with no spaces.
   - **Access hours** only appear once you turn on **Show access hours**.
3. Tap **Save**.

Change the phone number here once and it updates everywhere on the site — the
top bar, the buttons, the footer, all of it.

## What happens after you save

Your change is saved and the website rebuilds itself automatically. Give it
**1 to 2 minutes**, then refresh the page and you'll see it live. You don't need
to do anything else.

## If something looks wrong

- **Refresh the page** after a couple of minutes — it may still be updating.
- Nothing you type can break the site's design. If a value looks wrong on the
  site, go back into `/admin`, fix it, and save again.
- Every change is saved with a history, so nothing is ever truly lost.
